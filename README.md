# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple program that calculates simple interest based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

- Principal is the initial amount of money.
- Rate is the rate of interest in percentage.
- Time is the time period in years.

## Example

If the principal is 1000, the rate is 5%, and the time period is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = 100

## Inputs

- Principal amount
- Rate of interest
- Time period

## Output

The calculator displays the calculated simple interest.
